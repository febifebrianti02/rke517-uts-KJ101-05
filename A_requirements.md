# A. Understanding the client

Case KJ101-05 · Klinik Pratama Medika Utama, Jakarta Barat · about 90 visits a day, 2 medical records staff.
Client: Bapak Joko Santoso, Finance and Claims Officer. Sources: interview (Int), voice note (VN), paper form RM-51 (Form), staff chat (Chat), change request CR-1 (CR-1, 12 November 2026).

**Version v1.1.** Changes made for CR-1 are marked **[CR-1]**. Everything else is as in v1.0.

## Problem and goal
**Problem:** Outgoing referral letters are handled on paper. Patients wait about 40 minutes for a letter, the log book misses about one referral in five, and when a hospital calls to verify a letter the clinic cannot quickly find its copy. A hospital reply letter (for example Pak Udin's letter from the surgeon) has no place to be recorded at all.

**Goal:** An app where a referral letter is requested, approved, printed and logged in one place, so that nothing gets lost and everybody can see the status of every case at any time. Success in three months, in the client's words: he can see every case's status without asking anyone, and the problems above happen much less.

**[CR-1] Added in v1.1:** the app will be used by more people than planned, and it must also cover the branch clinic in the next village, which will use the same app. So there are now two sites (main clinic and branch clinic), and the goal above must hold for both.
